APERTURE — free photography portfolio template by uiCookies

Design concept: a "darkroom monochrome" portfolio that stays out of the photographs'
way. Instead of the usual cream-and-serif wedding look, Aperture leans cool and quiet —
near-black ink surfaces and a fog-grey paper — with a single restrained cyanotype-blue
accent borrowed from the classic photographic printing process. Chrome is deliberately
minimal so full-bleed imagery carries the page, and a film metaphor threads through the
whole thing: numbered frames and Space Mono EXIF captions (film stock, focal length,
aperture) sit on every gallery tile.

Palette:
  #15171E  ink (cool near-black)
  #20242E  graphite (dark surfaces / form)
  #EEF0F3  paper (cool light grey, blue bias — not cream)
  #767C89  fog (secondary text)
  #2F5D74  cyanotype blue (accent)
  #3E7590  cyan lift (hover / highlight)

Font pairing:
  Bodoni Moda — high-contrast Didone display, for headings and pull quotes (fashion-
                editorial character, the opposite of a generic sans hero).
  Archivo     — clean grotesque for body and UI.
  Space Mono  — technical monospace for eyebrows, EXIF captions and buttons.

Build: Bootstrap 5 (self-hosted), no jQuery, all interaction in hand-written vanilla JS
(js/main.js) — filterable masonry gallery, a from-scratch keyboard/swipe lightbox,
mobile nav, scroll reveal, animated counters, scrollspy, and front-end form validation.
Single page. Fully responsive, prefers-reduced-motion aware, skip link + semantic landmarks.
